# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Travelers planning a stay who want to compare hotel offers by destination, dates, guest count, nightly price, and rating.

## Product Purpose

Turn the existing hotel-search utility into a local web interface for finding and comparing stays, then exporting matching results. The app searches through its configured provider and does not sign in or make bookings.

## Brand Personality

Premium, editorial, considered. The interface should feel calm and trustworthy while helping travelers imagine a stay.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered online travel agency listings, noisy discount badges, and generic SaaS dashboard patterns.

## Design Principles

- Put the destination and trip details at the center of the search.
- Make useful comparison details easy to scan without crowding the results.
- Be clear about provider behavior and never imply that the app completes a booking.
- Make exporting results a straightforward continuation of the search.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard-accessible controls, clear contrast, and reduced-motion support.
